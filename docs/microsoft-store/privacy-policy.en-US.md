## Local data

ACECode stores model configuration, credentials, conversation history, workspace
information, preferences, and diagnostic logs on your device. On Windows, user
data is typically stored in the .acecode folder in your user directory. File
editing and command execution can also read or modify the workspaces and files
that the tools are allowed to access.

Uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete your workspaces, external
development tools, or all user data. Manage local records using the app's
management features and operating-system file tools. Revoke service credentials
with the relevant provider when you no longer want them to be usable.

## Model providers and extensions

AI features send prompts, relevant code, attachments, conversation context, and
tool results to the model service selected in your configuration. Credentials
are used to authenticate requests to that service. Processing location and
provider practices depend on your configuration and the provider's terms.

MCP integrations, skills, browser tools, Computer Use, remote connections, and
other extensions may access files, websites, screen contents, or external
services as needed for their functions. Review the permissions, recipients, and
task scope before use. Third-party retention and data-use practices are governed
by the providers' policies and your agreements with them.

## Feedback you choose to submit

Submitting feedback uploads your feedback text and recent diagnostic logs.
A selected conversation is included only when you choose to attach it.
The package may also include the application version, operating-system platform,
computer name, system login name, file paths, and task contents present in logs
or the selected conversation.

Feedback is used to investigate and fix problems. Do not submit code, credentials,
or personal information you do not wish to share. Feedback operator, server,
retention period, and deletion contact: [required]. Microsoft Store installations reject feedback uploads to non-HTTPS
addresses and display an explanation before any upload.

## Connections and protection

Depending on the features you use, the app connects to models, integrations,
websites, and other services. Use encrypted transport such as HTTPS for remote
services and protect your device, local configuration, and credentials.
Third-party retention or training practices are not guaranteed by this policy;
review the terms of the services you select.

## Your choices

You can choose providers, manage or delete local conversations, disable
integrations, change permissions, revoke service keys, and decide whether to
submit feedback or attach a conversation. Contact the operator above to request
access, correction, or deletion of uploaded feedback.

## Changes

The operator will update the published policy and its effective date when data
handling changes. New features must be reflected in the policy.
